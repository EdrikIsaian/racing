Put your own music files in this folder to replace the generated music.

  background.mp3   plays on the menu and during races
  menu.mp3         menu only (overrides background)
  race.mp3         races only (overrides background)

.mp3, .ogg, .wav and .flac all work. The music loops, fades between menu and race,
and M mutes it. Rebuild (or run with `dotnet run`) after adding a file so it's copied
next to the game.

Only use music you have the rights to (e.g. a track you bought or that is licensed
for this use); these files are not generated by the game.
